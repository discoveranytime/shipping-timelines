📦 Shipping guide

Shipping timelines

The question every new buyer asks first — when will my order ship? Here's what realistic delivery windows look like, why patience beats pestering, and what a delay actually means.

01

The big question

If you take one thing from this page, make it this.

"When will my order ship?" The most common question new buyers ask — and the answer is simpler than you'd think.

Once your payment is confirmed, your package will generally arrive inside the normal delivery window for the warehouse you picked. That's it. No secret steps, no hidden queue — confirmed payment plus the standard timeframe for your region.

The two things that set your wait are which warehouse your order ships from and whether anything outside anyone's control slows it down. Both are covered below.

02

USA warehouse

The fastest lane — domestic shipping, shortest wait.

3–7
Business days

Typical delivery window for orders shipping from a USA warehouse.

🚀 When to pick it

If you want your order as fast as possible, the domestic option is the clear pick. Shorter distance, no border crossing, fewer hands on the parcel.

The international lane — longer, but the majority land right on schedule.

10–15

Typical delivery window for orders shipping from a China warehouse.

🌍 Why it takes longer

International parcels travel farther and clear customs on the way in. That adds days by nature — but most orders still arrive comfortably inside this window.

Order confirmed~2–3 weeksDelivered
04

Don't rush your vendor

Patience isn't just polite here — it's the fastest strategy.

📨 Tracking comes to you

After payment confirmation, there's no need to keep asking your representative for tracking updates. Representatives process hundreds, sometimes thousands of orders a day — your tracking information goes out as soon as it's available.

Sending message after message asking "where's my tracking?" doesn't move your parcel any faster. It just piles more work onto the rep, which slows down responses for everyone — including you.

❌ What doesn't help

  • Repeated "any update?" messages
  • Messaging on multiple channels at once
  • Assuming silence means something's wrong

✅ What does help

  • One clear order, confirmed once
  • Waiting for the tracking message to arrive
  • Checking the tracking number yourself first
05

Delays happen

Sometimes an order needs a few extra days. Here's what usually causes it.

🏭

Factory production delays

If stock is being manufactured or restocked, your order waits for the production run to finish.

🚚

Carrier delays

Shipping companies hit backlogs, weather, and peak-season pileups like everyone else.

🛃

Customs processing

International parcels get inspected at the border. Routine, but it adds days you can't control.

🗺️

Route changes

International shipping routes shift from time to time, rerouting parcels mid-journey.

This kind of slowdown is normal from time to time and usually isn't a cause for concern. A few extra days on the warehouse's standard window is part of international shipping — not a sign your order is lost.

06

The bottom line

The whole page in one paragraph.

Payment confirmed? Be patient and trust the process.

  • The vast majority of orders arrive without any issue at all.
  • Domestic orders typically land in under a week; international ones in two to three weeks.
  • Give your representative time to process — tracking arrives as soon as it exists.
  • We're used to next-day everything, but good things take time. It'll be worth the wait.
