# Shipping Timelines — What to Expect After You Order

A single self-contained interactive HTML infographic (no build step, no backend). Open `index.html` in any browser.

Plain-language guide to delivery expectations: USA warehouse 3–7 business days, China warehouse 10–15 business days, why patience beats pestering your vendor, what delays actually mean, and key terms. Amber/orange/cyan on dark, with light mode, sidebar navigation, and a rating quiz.

Educational reference only — delivery timeframes are estimates, not guarantees.
